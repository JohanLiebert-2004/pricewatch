About Dealwatch

A clearer view of Australian retail prices, built to help anyone check a deal before buying.

How prices and discounts are checkedContact and corrections

Free to use

Anyone can browse Dealwatch, compare recorded prices and view price history. No Dealwatch account is needed. Price alerts are optional.

No affiliate links

Dealwatch does not use affiliate links or earn affiliate commissions from your purchases. When you follow a retailer link, you buy directly from that retailer.

An independent project

Dealwatch started as a personal project to make price changes easier to follow. It is not a retailer and does not sell products or handle payments.

Look beyond the discount badge

An advertised RRP and a previously recorded selling price are different reference points. Dealwatch labels the comparison behind a discount and shows when prices were checked. Recorded history starts when we first track a listing; it is not a complete record of every price a store has ever charged.

Compare the same product

On product pages, the barcode comparison lists other retailers with an equivalent valid barcode. You can see the item price, marketplace label and check date together. Model-code and similar-product suggestions appear separately because they may refer to different variants or packs.

Delivery, local stock and retailer-supplied data can change the comparison. Always confirm the exact product and total price with the retailer.

Optional alerts

Set an email target on a product page and confirm the email we send to activate the alert. Telegram links offer another way to follow products or retailer deals. Read how alert information is handled.

Help correct the record

Use “Report an incorrect price” on a product page, or contact Dealwatch with the listing link and what looks wrong. Prices and availability can change between checks, and coverage varies between retailers.
